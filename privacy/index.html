CarpIQ About & Privacy

Updated 14 October 2026. Feature availability depends on the app version installed. Catch Camera Pro and the current features described here require the latest CarpIQ update.

Basic access and age restrictions

Basic has the same free features for all ages: weather and free widgets, a private catch log and personal Trophy Wall, map search and directions, and fishing guides. Session Diary, planning, saved fisheries, saved spots and other Pro extras require an active Pro trial or subscription; age information never unlocks them for free. Under-18s and users whose age information is unavailable or declined can continue using Basic, but cannot start new Pro trials or purchases, add mates or share catches/photos. Existing purchases can still be restored; sharing remains subject to adult age access. Saved records are retained when Pro access ends. CarpIQ asks Apple for an age band, not a date of birth. Apple's information may be self-declared or supplied by a parent; it is not always independently verified. An adult age range enables the adult experience; it does not start a subscription. After the first age request, CarpIQ silently checks a recent saved result when the app becomes active. An Apple age request is shown when you choose to check age for restricted features, not on every launch. A device-only Keychain record stores the adult result, check time and an app-specific iCloud account identifier for up to 24 hours of background sharing. The current iCloud account must match. This record cannot start purchases; unavailable or expired background access waits until the app checks Apple again. Children should review this information with a parent or guardian.

Existing sharing and subscriptions

Switching to private mode blocks new sharing but does not automatically revoke previous board access, delete records already shared, or cancel an Apple subscription. Use Review previous board connections to remove existing connections, and Apple's subscription settings to cancel an existing plan. Remote removal needs an internet connection and may take time. Downloaded photos, screenshots and exported messages cannot be recalled.

Hands-free Catch Camera

Catch Camera requires an active Pro trial or subscription. Ordinary camera photos and album uploads remain free. Ending Pro access does not delete saved catches, catch photos or retained camera drafts. Catch Camera uses the camera while its screen is open. Optional voice commands use microphone and speech permissions and require on-device recognition; audio is not sent to a CarpIQ server or saved by CarpIQ. If local voice recognition is unavailable, use the timer. Face-quality guidance is processed on this phone and does not identify you or fish species, or reliably check the whole fish. Camera shots are saved in a separate local draft folder and retained when you close the camera. You can select multiple camera shots and delete them after confirmation. On Close, choose Save to Photos and close, Delete and close, or Keep for later. Album saving requests add-only Photos permission; camera copies are removed only after Apple confirms the album save. A selected camera set is cleaned up only after the catch saves successfully with the chosen photo. Failed or cancelled saves retain camera copies; saved catch photos and exported copies are not deleted. Older shots without a reliable set identifier are cleaned up individually rather than grouped by guesswork. Choosing a shot adds it to your unsaved catch draft; it is shared or backed up as a catch photo only through the existing catch-save flow. Camera drafts may be included in device backups; deleting the app removes local drafts. Backgrounding the app stops camera and voice capture.

Storage and iCloud sync

Catch logs, photos, profile and mate records are stored on this device, with private iCloud backup/sync when available. Adult Trophy Board sharing uses Apple's CloudKit sharing service. Session Diary is saved on this phone and is not shared with mates or included in CarpIQ's manual iCloud backup; device backups may include it. Sync needs an available iCloud account and a connection. iOS controls background execution, and changes can be delayed or fail. Check any error shown and retry; a delay does not prove everything is working normally. Keep your own backups of important records.

Who can see shared information

In adult mode, new catches are automatically queued to your Trophy Wall. Connected board participants can see shared catches, photos and membership details. Shared information is not public to every CarpIQ user, but recipients can save or forward it. Review your connections and what a catch contains, including locations and notes, before sharing. A profile name is not proof of someone's identity. Private mode does not upload new catches to these shared boards.

Developer access and security

Private records are stored on your device and in your private Apple iCloud storage, not on a CarpIQ-operated personal-records server. The developer does not routinely receive your private catch photos or diary. Information you deliberately send to support, including an abuse report or a board invitation, can be seen by its recipient. Apple may provide the developer with sales, transaction and diagnostic information. Apple protects iCloud through its security measures and privacy terms, but no service can guarantee protection against every compromise. Protect your Apple Account and devices. This notice does not remove rights you have under applicable law.

Location and external providers

With permission, phone coordinates are used for local weather, maps and nearby places. Weather and air-quality requests send coordinates to Open-Meteo; maps/place search use Apple services. Nearby-fishery lookup uses an OpenStreetMap Overpass service; sun-time lookup uses sunrise-sunset.org. Radar loads RainViewer imagery, OpenStreetMap tiles and Leaflet resources from unpkg.com. Providers receive requests and normal network information such as your IP address. External links are governed by the destination service. You can change location/photo permissions in iPhone Settings.

Widgets and weather alerts

The app and widgets share weather, coordinates and limited session details through an Apple App Group on this device. Catches, photos, notes and mate records are not copied to that widget store. Widgets request location independently when allowed. iOS controls timing: unlocking the phone is not a guaranteed refresh trigger. Use the widget refresh control and check its location/update time. Location-specific weather alerts require a fresh location and forecast immediately before requesting delivery; otherwise they are skipped. Future location-specific alerts are not queued. iOS controls background checks, so alerts may be delayed or missed. Morning and evening reminders contain no stored forecast or location. Focus and notification summaries may delay presentation after sending. Saved fisheries provide forecasts on demand, not automatic venue alerts.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and can reveal weather, session or adult catch details on the lock screen. Change them in Notifications and in iPhone Settings. Focus, notification summaries, permissions and iOS background scheduling affect delivery. Forecasts, warnings, fishing scores and estimated water temperatures are guidance, not guarantees or safety advice.

Adult Pro trial and billing

The adult app trial lasts 28 days, takes no payment and does not automatically start a subscription. Paid plans use Apple In-App Purchase; Apple presents the price before confirmation and manages billing, renewal and cancellation. Starting a paid plan can charge immediately, including during the separate app trial. Trial dates/expiry state are stored on this device, in Keychain and private iCloud key-value storage when available. Ending Pro locks adult paid features rather than deleting saved data. Existing records can become available again with valid access, but deleted records or other people's removed shares cannot be restored by paying. Basic does not create a subscription or include free Pro extras based on age.

Messages, reporting and blocking

Adult catch exports use the messaging or sharing service you select. The recipient and service can retain copies. Remove unwanted board connections inside CarpIQ; iPhone Messages/Phone blocking does not remove CloudKit access. Send concerns to carpiq@icloud.com and include only information you want support to receive. CarpIQ currently provides connection removal and support reporting; do not assume it has a comprehensive automated moderation or persistent account-blocking system.

Your choices, deletion and contact

You can edit/delete local catches, diary entries, spots and mates. Device records, backups and shared records are separate: deleting the app may leave iCloud data that restores later. Remove board access before clearing data, and review iCloud storage in iPhone Settings. Other people's saved copies are not deleted by these actions. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent to the developer, contact carpiq@icloud.com. The developer cannot retrieve your private iCloud records on your behalf.
