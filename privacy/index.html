🎣 CarpIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

CarpIQ is a weather, mapping and catch-logging companion app for carp anglers, built by an independent developer.

🔒 Your Data

Your catches, saved fisheries, photos and profile are stored in your own personal iCloud account. We cannot see or access this data — it belongs entirely to you and is encrypted by Apple.

📍 Location & Weather Data

CarpIQ uses your device's location to show live weather, pressure, wind and pollen for where you are, and to power the fishery map and search. Your location is used live to fetch this information and is not stored, tracked over time, or sent anywhere beyond the weather request itself.

🚫 No Tracking or Analytics

CarpIQ does not track your activity, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not show ads. We have no visibility into how you use the app.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Weather and forecast data is provided by Apple WeatherKit. Pollen and some map weather data is provided by Open-Meteo. Fishery search and directions use Apple Maps. These services process only the location data needed to return results and are governed by their own privacy policies.

🤝 Sharing With Fishing Mates

CarpIQ lets you save fishing buddies by name and phone number in the Mates list. When you share a catch, you choose who to send it to, and CarpIQ opens iMessage with the details pre-filled for you to send. A shared catch includes the photo, species, weight, venue, bait, rig, moon phase and a map link to the catch location.

This data leaves CarpIQ once sent. It is not stored on any CarpIQ server and we cannot access, retrieve, or delete it after it has been sent — it becomes a normal message on the recipient's device, governed by that messaging app's own privacy policy.

⚠️ Sharing & Content Responsibility

You are solely responsible for the content you share through CarpIQ, including photos, notes and messages. Do not share inappropriate, offensive, unlawful, or copyrighted content. Misuse of the sharing features may result in being blocked from the app and reported.

🚫 If Someone Bothers You

CarpIQ does not use central accounts, so blocking is managed directly on your device: remove them from your Fishing Mates list at any time, or block their number in iOS via Messages or Phone. This stops all contact, including anything shared via CarpIQ. You can also report persistent issues to us using the contact details below.

🗑️ Delete Your Data

To delete all your CarpIQ data, go to iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → CarpIQ → Delete Data. You can also delete the app itself at any time.

📬 Contact

For any privacy questions or to exercise your data rights, contact us at carpiq@icloud.com.
