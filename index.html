CarpIQ Pro

These features require an active Pro trial or subscription. New trials and purchases require an adult age range (18+); Fishing Mates and catch sharing also require adult age access.

  • Hands-free Catch Camera: Fishing alone? Use voice commands or a 10-second timer to take three photos. Spoken countdowns and on-device face guidance help you get ready. Review, rotate, save to Photos or delete shots, then choose one for your catch. Voice commands require supported on-device speech recognition. Check the whole fish is in frame yourself.
  • Session Diary & Separate Trips: See upcoming, active and past trips with countdowns. Each trip has its own editable dates, venue, notes, shopping and packing lists, directions and reminders. Copy a previous trip. Saved privately on this phone.
  • Per-Session Weather Outlook: Open any session to see daily and hourly weather, moon phase, pressure when available, rain, wind and warnings for each day of your trip. Plan any date; forecasts cover only the next 10 days and explain when later dates are expected to become available.
  • Widget Session Countdown: See your next session countdown on CarpIQ’s small, medium and large Home Screen widgets. Tap the session area to open that trip. Weather and fishing-score widgets are also available in Basic; iOS controls refresh timing.
  • Session Reminders: Choose weekly countdowns, daily reminders in the final 7 days and an arrival-time reminder. Requires notification permission; open CarpIQ regularly to queue later reminders. Focus and iPhone settings affect delivery.
  • Fishing Mates: Manage your fishing mates and connect through Trophy Boards. Fishing Mates requires Pro; Basic includes a personal Trophy Wall for your own catches.
  • Connected Trophy Boards: Pro lets you connect Trophy Boards and see your mates’ shared catches alongside your own. New Catch Log entries automatically go to your board. Basic shows only your own catches; existing sharing connections remain until removed.
  • Solunar Feeding Tables: Estimated moon-based feeding windows for the days ahead; not a guarantee of catches.
  • Optimal Fishing Windows: Estimated 2–3 hour fishing windows using weather and estimated water conditions, with moon information for context.
  • Catch Pattern Analysis: Spot the conditions that produce your best catches, built from your own logged history.
  • Pressure Drop Alerts: Advance alerts for significant forecast pressure drops at the location checked during a fresh weather refresh. Each alert names that location; it may be your previous location if you travel.
  • Estimated Water Temperature: Current estimated water temperature based on recent air-temperature trends.
  • Lake Notes: Save map pins for your fishing spots, depth, snags, margins and features, with your own notes.
  • Catch Photo Sharing: Share your catch photo and full report with fishing mates via Messages, WhatsApp or email.
  • Saved Fisheries: Bookmark venues from search or the map, with full venue weather, quick directions and one-tap Show on Map. Venue weather does not send automatic alerts.
  • Nearby Fisheries: Discover fisheries around your current location.
  • 10-Day Forecast: Full 10-day in-app outlook, up from 3 days in the Basic app. Weather-only widgets offer up to 10 days for everyone.
  • 5-Day Pressure Outlook: Extended pressure trend, up from 3 days on Basic.
  • 5-Day Seasonal Pollen Estimate: Seasonal estimates, not measured local pollen counts. Five days instead of three on Basic.
  • 10-Day Moon Outlook: See moon phase and estimated bite rating for the next 10 days at a glance.

Trial, subscriptions and saved information

Adults can start a 28-day Pro app trial. It takes no payment and does not automatically start a subscription. A paid Apple subscription is a separate purchase. Ordinary camera photos, album uploads, your Catch Log and personal Trophy Wall remain free. Ending Pro access does not delete saved catches, catch photos or retained camera drafts. Saved Pro information remains locked until valid access is restored, if that information is still retained.
